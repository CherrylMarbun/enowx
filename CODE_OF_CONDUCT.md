# Code of Conduct

enowX is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. We want working on it to be welcoming and productive for all of them.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Be clear and constructive in reviews: say what to change and why.
- Help newcomers find their way; nobody starts out knowing the codebase.
- Respect people's time: keep issues and pull requests focused, and follow up on what you open.

## What isn't accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language, in any space of the project.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews), in the project's chat spaces, and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to the maintainers privately, through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting or by email to the address on the maintainers' profiles. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban people who don't follow it.
